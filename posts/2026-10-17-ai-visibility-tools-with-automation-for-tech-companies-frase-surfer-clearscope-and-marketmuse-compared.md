## How do Frase, Surfer, Clearscope and MarketMuse compare at a glance?

The four tools do different jobs. Frase covers research, drafting, publishing and AI visibility tracking in one product, Surfer pairs a content editor with an AI Tracker, Clearscope centres on topic grading and query tracking, and MarketMuse centres on topic research and content briefs. The table below uses each vendor's own wording, and "Not listed" means the pages read for this article did not mention the item.

## What does Frase offer for AI visibility and automation?

Frase combines content research, AI drafting, optimization, publishing and AI visibility tracking in one product, and it describes itself as "The content operating system for AI search". Its product menu lists SEO Research, Topic Clusters, an AI Agent, Brand Voice, Content Optimization, GEO Optimization, AI Visibility, Site Audit, FraseCMS, Content Guard and Backlinks.

For AI visibility, Frase's menu describes the feature as "Monitor where AI search cites you". The pricing page lists the engines by plan: ChatGPT and Google AI on Starter, Perplexity added on Professional, and Claude and Gemini added on Scale. The prompt allowances are 50 on Starter, 200 on Professional and 500 on Scale, and history is kept for 3 months on Professional and 12 months on Scale, while Starter shows current data only. The FAQ on Frase's home page says the tracking runs daily and alerts you when share of voice moves.

On automation, Frase says: "Let Frase publish on its own, keep every page under review, or anywhere in between. You set the dial." It can publish to WordPress, Webflow, Sanity and Wix, or to FraseCMS, which it describes as "an agent-ready CMS, hosted or headless, on your own domain". An MCP server, CLI and API are available on every plan, with monthly request limits of 5,000 on Starter, 20,000 on Professional and 50,000 on Scale. Frase also says it can "Write natively in 70+ languages, never translated."

Two details on the pricing page matter for budgeting. Starter "simply stops at its monthly limits, so a surprise bill is off the table", while on Professional and Scale pay-as-you-go is optional and off by default. Enterprise adds SSO and SAML, white-label reports, a branded client portal, and a dedicated account manager and SLA.

### What do Frase's plans include?

- Starter: $39 per month billed yearly or $49 month to month; 1 seat, 1 site, 10 articles and 50 audit pages each month.
- Professional: $103 per month billed yearly or $129 month to month; 3 seats, 5 sites, 40 articles and 250 audit pages each month; extra seats cost $29 per month.
- Scale: $239 per month billed yearly or $299 month to month; 5 seats, up to 10 domains, 100 articles and 1,000 audit pages each month.
- Enterprise: custom pricing and custom limits.
- Trial: a free 7-day trial with no credit card, with Professional features capped at 5 articles, 50 audit pages, 25 AI prompts and up to 3 seats.

## What does Surfer offer for AI visibility and automation?

Surfer pairs a Content Editor with an AI Tracker. Its Content Score is "a real-time responsive metric that shows you if you’re on track for optimization", and the Content Editor page cites "500+ ranking factors analyzed". Organic Competitors in the editor are pages that rank in the top 10 of Google for the chosen location, and top-cited pages in AI answers also count.

Surfer lists several automatic features on the Content Editor page. "Auto-Optimize analyzes your content against your competitors to find opportunities for boosting your Content Score", and "Internal Linking scans your domain, finds the most optimal anchor, and inserts internal links automatically." The editor's guidelines can be used in Google Docs, ChatGPT, WordPress or Contentful. On the pricing page, WordPress and Google Docs appear from the Standard plan, Contentful from the Pro plan, and Zapier and API Access on the Peace of Mind plan. Surfer's home page adds that the API is "only available to active users with the API add-on or users on an Enterprise plan", so confirm the API terms with Surfer before relying on them.

The AI Tracker page lists Gemini, ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, AI Mode and Perplexity, and promises to "See Real AI Share of Voice" and to "Find & Fix Mention Gaps". Its FAQ says the Standard plan tracks ChatGPT only, while Pro and Peace of Mind cover all supported models. Prompt checks run on a schedule that depends on the plan, and Surfer's wording is "Set it once. Stay in the loop without lifting a finger."

### What are Surfer's main plans?

- Discovery: 49 USD per month billed yearly; "Create or Optimize 120 Documents", "Track 10 Pages" and "Track 0 AI prompts".
- Standard: 99 USD per month billed yearly; 360 documents and 25 AI prompts refreshed weekly.
- Pro: 182 USD per month billed yearly; 360 documents and 50 AI prompts refreshed daily.
- Peace of Mind: 299 USD per month billed yearly; unlimited documents (marked with an asterisk on the pricing page) and 100 AI prompts refreshed daily.
- AI Search Analytics: 82 USD per month billed yearly; 50 AI prompts, described as "Track your brand's visibility across ChatGPT, Gemini, AI Overviews, and other AI models."
- Enterprise: custom pricing.
- Trial: 7 days of full access to the Pro plan; billing details are entered at checkout, the trial converts to the selected plan, and "The trial does not cancel automatically."

## What does Clearscope offer for AI visibility and automation?

Clearscope focuses on topic coverage and query tracking. Its Optimize page describes "Instant feedback on topic coverage and content score as you draft" and names Google Docs, Microsoft Word and WordPress as writing tools. Its home page also lists an AI drafting and editing workflow that it says "helps you accelerate your first pass", and the Essentials and Business plans each include 20 monthly Drafts.

For AI visibility, the pricing page says every plan includes Query Tracking: "Track specific queries across ChatGPT and Gemini". Clearscope's home page describes the same feature as showing how your brand is mentioned "across ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude" and where competitors are recommended instead. Because the two pages name different assistants, ask Clearscope which ones your plan covers. Essentials tracks 50 queries and Business tracks 300.

Clearscope also sells research and site-level analytics. Its Query Discovery tool lets you "perform high-level research by entering a topic or website URL", and its Protect product offers "powerful content analytics for your entire site or individual pages". The help center documents a WordPress integration, a Google Docs integration and a Google Search Console connection. None of the Clearscope pages read for this article mentions an API.

On team size, Clearscope's pricing page lists "Unlimited" users on every plan. The Business plan adds a dedicated account manager, and Enterprise adds single sign-on, custom agreements and crawler whitelisting.

### What do Clearscope's plans include?

- Essentials: $129/month; 50 tracked queries, 50 pages, 20 monthly topic explorations and 20 monthly drafts.
- Business: $399/month; 300 tracked queries, 300 pages, 50 monthly topic explorations, 20 monthly drafts and a dedicated account manager.
- Enterprise: custom pricing, with custom credits, SSO, custom agreements and crawler whitelisting.
- Every plan: unlimited users and a 14 day free trial.

## What does MarketMuse offer for planning and AI visibility?

MarketMuse centres on topic planning and research. Its home page lists Competitive Content Analysis, Content Clusters, Content Planning, a Content Brief Generator and Optimize, which includes a generative AI component. It says the platform "pinpoints high-value topic clusters and finds quick wins based on your existing authority" and gives "a personalized roadmap showing what to create or update". It also states: "We don't act like or replace a CMS."

MarketMuse content briefs give writers "structure, related topics, questions to answer and linking suggestions". The briefs page lists nine types: Article, News, Local, Comparison, FAQ, How-to, Product Review, Listicle and Guide. A brief can be customized with 15 inputs, such as point of view, tone and target market, and exported to Google Docs or MS Word.

None of the MarketMuse pages read for this article (home page, pricing page and content briefs page) describes tracking of AI assistants or AI answers, and none mentions an API. If AI visibility tracking is your main need, MarketMuse is therefore not the obvious fit on the evidence published. MarketMuse publishes no paid prices: every plan says "Book a demo".

### What are MarketMuse's plans?

- Free: 1 user and 10 queries/month; "Best for individuals with a low content volume."
- Optimize: 1 user, 100 tracked topics, 5 content briefs a month and 100 queries a month; "Best for individuals publishing content regularly."
- Research: 3 users, 1000 tracked topics, 10 content briefs a month and unlimited queries; "Best for teams looking to add rocket fuel to their content output."
- Strategy: 5 users, 10K tracked topics, 20 content briefs a month and unlimited queries; "Best for large teams and agencies scaling their content planning."

## Which of these tools track AI assistants, and how far do they go?

Frase, Surfer and Clearscope each describe AI visibility tracking on their own pages, and the pages read for MarketMuse do not. The coverage and limits differ by plan, so the useful comparison is the number of tracked prompts or queries, the assistants covered and how often the data refreshes.

### How do the AI tracking allowances compare?

- Frase: 50 prompts on Starter, 200 on Professional and 500 on Scale; ChatGPT and Google AI on Starter, up to Claude and Gemini on Scale.
- Surfer: 0 AI prompts on Discovery, 25 refreshed weekly on Standard, 50 refreshed daily on Pro and 100 refreshed daily on Peace of Mind; the separate AI Search Analytics plan tracks 50.
- Clearscope: 50 tracked queries on Essentials and 300 on Business, across ChatGPT and Gemini according to the pricing page.
- MarketMuse: not listed on the pages read.

Frase and Surfer count prompts while Clearscope counts queries, so treat these numbers as the limits of each product and not as like-for-like measures of coverage.

## What does Google say about appearing in AI Overviews and AI Mode?

Google says there are no special requirements for AI features beyond being in Search: its documentation states, "There are no additional requirements to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode, nor other special optimizations necessary." A page "must be indexed and eligible to be shown in Google Search with a snippet".

Google also notes that "Indexing and serving isn't guaranteed", and that sites appearing in AI features are included in overall search traffic and "reported on in the Performance report" in Search Console under the "Web" search type. For a buyer, this means an AI visibility score from any of these tools measures mentions for the prompts you chose to track. It does not promise a position in Google's AI features.

## How can you test these tools before you pay?

All four let you try something before committing, but the terms differ. Frase offers a free 7-day trial with no credit card. Surfer gives 7 days of its Pro plan, asks for billing details at checkout and converts the trial to the plan you picked unless you cancel. Clearscope offers a 14 day free trial on all plans. MarketMuse has a Free plan with 1 user and 10 queries a month.

Use the trial on real work. Add the prompts or queries you actually care about, publish or export one article through the integration you would use every day, and invite the people who would log in. If you test Surfer, note the cancellation steps on day one, because its help center warns that the trial does not cancel automatically.

## How should a tech company choose between them?

Start with the job to be done and then compare limits. If you want research, drafting, publishing and AI tracking in one subscription, Frase lists all of them in its menu. If your writers already work in Google Docs or WordPress, Surfer and Clearscope both list those integrations. If your main need is deciding what to write next, MarketMuse's topic clusters, briefs and roadmap are built for that, and it does not replace a CMS.

Then compare team size and budget. Clearscope lists unlimited users on every plan at $129/month for Essentials. Frase Starter has 1 seat at $39 per month billed yearly, with extra seats at $29 per month on Professional and Scale. MarketMuse prices are available only through a demo. Surfer's cheapest plan, Discovery at 49 USD per month, tracks 0 AI prompts, so a team that wants AI tracking should compare Standard at 99 USD per month or the AI Search Analytics plan at 82 USD per month.

### What should be on your buying checklist?

- Which AI assistants you need covered, and whether your plan includes them.
- How many prompts or queries you will track, and how often they refresh.
- Which CMS or editor you publish from, and whether the integration is on your plan.
- How many people need a login, and what extra seats cost.
- Whether you need API or MCP access, and which plan includes it.
- Trial length, billing details and how to cancel.
