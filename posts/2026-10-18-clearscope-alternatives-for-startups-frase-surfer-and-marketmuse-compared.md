# Clearscope Alternatives for Startups: Frase, Surfer and MarketMuse Compared

Clearscope is one of several tools that help teams write, optimize and track content for Google and for AI assistants such as ChatGPT and Gemini. This article compares it with three alternatives that a small team can start with: Frase, Surfer and MarketMuse. It looks at what each vendor publishes about prices, free trials, content scoring, AI visibility tracking and integrations, so that a startup can match a tool to its budget and its workflow. All figures and features come from the vendors' own pages, read in October 2026. Vendors change plans often, so check the linked pages before you buy.

Its home page lists modules named Expand, Discover, Write, Optimize, Protect, Localize and Linking, plus Query Tracking that shows where a brand is mentioned in ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude and where competitors are recommended instead. Its pricing page also lists a 14 day free trial, free training and onboarding, priority support and plan changes with no contracts. None of that is a reason to avoid it. The point of a comparison is to see which set of features and which price suits your team, and each of the four tools below makes different trade-offs.

## What does Frase offer a small team?

Frase combines content research, AI drafting, optimization and AI visibility tracking in one product, and it publishes to several content management systems. Its plans start with Starter at $39 per month billed yearly, or $49 month to month.

On its home page Frase describes its research as "Content research that ends in a brief", and it offers Topic Clusters for mapping the content around a topic. Its AI Agent drafts with your brand context, and the drafts are checked against the pages that already rank. Each page gets a search score and an AI-readiness score, and the Content Optimization feature is described as "Score and lift pages for Google", while GEO Optimization is meant to tune content so that AI tools cite it. Frase also says it monitors where AI search cites your brand across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI daily, and tracks your share of voice.

The pricing page sets limits by plan. Starter has 1 seat and 1 site, 10 articles and 50 audit pages each month, and 50 AI Visibility prompts. Professional, at $103 per month billed yearly or $129 month to month, has 3 seats and 5 sites, 40 articles and 250 audit pages each month, and 200 prompts. Scale, at $239 per month billed yearly or $299 month to month, has 5 seats and up to 10 domains, 100 articles and 1,000 audit pages each month, and 500 prompts. Extra seats cost $29 per month on Professional and Scale. Enterprise is custom.

Two details help with budgeting. The pricing page says "Starter simply stops at its monthly limits, so a surprise bill is off the table", while pay-as-you-go on Professional and Scale is optional and off by default. The AI Visibility engines also depend on the plan: ChatGPT and Google AI on Starter, with Perplexity added on Professional and Claude and Gemini added on Scale. History is kept for 3 months on Professional and 12 months on Scale, and Starter shows current data only.

### What are the key facts about Frase?

- Starter: $39 per month billed yearly or $49 month to month, with 1 seat, 1 site, 10 articles and 50 audit pages each month.
- Professional: $103 per month billed yearly or $129 month to month, with 3 seats, 5 sites, 40 articles and 250 audit pages each month.
- Scale: $239 per month billed yearly or $299 month to month, with 5 seats, up to 10 domains, 100 articles and 1,000 audit pages each month.
- Trial: a free 7-day trial with no credit card, capped at 5 articles, 50 audit pages, 25 AI prompts and up to 3 seats.
- Publishing: WordPress, Webflow, Sanity and Wix, or its own FraseCMS.

## How does Surfer compare with Clearscope?

Surfer pairs a Content Editor with AI visibility tracking, and its plans start at 49 USD per month billed yearly, below Clearscope's $129/month. The two tools overlap on real-time content scoring and on integrations with writing tools, but they package tracking differently.

Surfer's Content Editor page describes the Content Score as "a real-time responsive metric that shows you if you're on track for optimization" and cites "500+ ranking factors analyzed". It says Auto-Optimize "analyzes your content against your competitors to find opportunities", and that Internal Linking "scans your domain, finds the most optimal anchor, and inserts internal links automatically". Organic Competitors in the editor are pages that rank in the top 10 of Google for the chosen SERP location. The editor works with Google Docs, ChatGPT, WordPress and Contentful.

The pricing page lists the plans, all billed yearly. Discovery is 49 USD per month for 120 documents, tracking of 10 pages and AI SEO optimization guidelines, with "Track 0 AI prompts". Standard is 99 USD per month for 360 documents and 25 AI prompts tracked across ChatGPT, refreshed weekly, and it adds the WordPress and Google Docs integrations, 1-click content optimization, team collaboration, a plagiarism checker and rank drop detection. Pro is 182 USD per month and tracks 50 prompts refreshed daily across all models. Peace of Mind is 299 USD per month, with 100 prompts refreshed daily, unlimited documents (marked with an asterisk), Advanced SERP Analysis, API access and a dedicated success manager. A separate AI Search Analytics plan is 82 USD per month and tracks 50 prompts refreshed daily across ChatGPT, Gemini, AI Overviews and other AI models.

Check the trial terms before signing up. Surfer's help center says that for 7 days you get full access to the Pro plan, that your card is charged for the first billing period when the trial ends, and that "The trial does not cancel automatically". The pricing page also says annual plans are charged for the entire year.

### What are the key facts about Surfer?

- Discovery: 49 USD per month billed yearly, with 120 documents, 10 tracked pages and no AI prompt tracking.
- Standard: 99 USD per month billed yearly, with 360 documents and 25 AI prompts refreshed weekly across ChatGPT.
- Pro: 182 USD per month billed yearly, with 50 AI prompts refreshed daily.
- Trial: 7 days of Pro, billing details required, and it does not cancel automatically.
- Integrations: WordPress and Google Docs from Standard, Contentful on Pro.

Enterprise adds custom credits, crawler whitelisting and single sign-on.

This gives a startup something to measure alternatives against. ## What are the pros and cons of MarketMuse for a startup?

MarketMuse is built around topic research and content briefs, and it publishes no prices. Its pricing page shows four plans, Free, Optimize, Research and Strategy, and each one leads to "Book a demo" rather than a price.

The Free plan has 1 user and 10 queries per month and is described as "Best for individuals with a low content volume." Optimize has 1 user, 100 tracked topics, 5 briefs and 100 queries per month. Research has 3 users, 1,000 tracked topics and 10 briefs per month with unlimited queries. Strategy has 5 users, 10K tracked topics and 20 briefs per month with unlimited queries, and is described as "Best for large teams and agencies scaling their content planning."

The product pages describe what the tool does. Its home page says "We locate gaps in your competitor's content and show you topics they've missed" and that its AI "pinpoints high-value topic clusters and finds quick wins based on your existing authority". Its content briefs page says briefs "provide all of the research that underlies the outline" and offers nine brief types: Article, News, Local, Comparison, FAQ, How-to, Product Review, Listicle and Guide. Briefs can be exported to Google Docs or Microsoft Word, and the page does not mention content management system integrations.

For a startup, the advantage that can be stated from these pages is the Free plan, which lets a team try the topic research before talking to sales. The practical drawback is that without published prices a team cannot compare cost until it books a demo. The pricing and product pages read did not list AI visibility tracking, so a team that needs it should ask MarketMuse directly.

### What are the key facts about MarketMuse?

- No prices are published; every plan points to "Book a demo".
- The Free plan has 1 user and 10 queries per month.
- Optimize includes 100 tracked topics and 5 briefs per month; Research includes 1,000 tracked topics and 10 briefs per month.
- Nine brief types, exportable to Google Docs or Microsoft Word.
- AI visibility tracking is not listed on the pages read.

## How should a startup choose between these tools?

Start with the job you need done each month, then check which plan covers it at a price you can sustain. The four tools solve different problems, so the cheapest one is not automatically the right one.

If the main need is a single product for research, drafting, publishing and AI tracking, Frase's Starter plan is listed at $39 per month billed yearly, and its limits of 10 articles and 50 audit pages each month show quickly whether you will outgrow it. If the main need is an editor with a live score for writers who work in Google Docs or WordPress, Surfer and Clearscope both describe that workflow, at 49 USD and $129/month respectively for the entry plans. If the need is planning topics for a larger content programme, MarketMuse's briefs and topic research are the focus, and a demo is the only way to learn the price.

Also check the trial terms. Frase offers a free 7-day trial with no credit card, Clearscope lists a 14 day free trial, MarketMuse has a Free plan, and Surfer's 7-day Pro trial needs billing details and does not cancel automatically. A trial is the most reliable way to test whether a content score actually changes how your writers work.

### What should you check before you subscribe?

- Which AI engines the plan tracks, and how many prompts or queries it includes.
- Whether the price you see is billed yearly or month to month.
- Whether the trial needs a card and whether it cancels on its own.
- Whether the tool connects to the tools your writers already use, such as Google Docs, Microsoft Word or WordPress.
- Which limits you would reach first, such as articles, pages, documents or seats.

## Does AI visibility need a separate tool?

Not necessarily: Google says there are no special requirements for appearing in its AI features, so a tool is a way to measure and plan rather than a requirement. Its documentation says "There are no additional requirements to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode, nor other special optimizations necessary."

Google also says a page must be indexed and eligible to be shown in Google Search with a snippet to be a supporting link, and that traffic from AI features counts toward overall search traffic and is reported in the Search Console Performance report. A startup can therefore start with Google's Search Console reporting and add a paid tool when it needs to track mentions in assistants such as ChatGPT or Gemini, or to score drafts against competing pages.

The vendors differ in how much tracking they include. Surfer's Discovery plan tracks no AI prompts, Frase tracks 50 prompts on Starter, and Clearscope lists query tracking across ChatGPT and Gemini on every plan. That is a reason to read the plan limits rather than the product name.

## FAQs

### What is an AI visibility platform?

It is software that shows whether and how a brand is mentioned in AI assistants and AI search results, and in some cases helps create or optimize content. Clearscope, for example, describes Query Tracking that shows where a brand is mentioned in ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude, and Surfer describes tracking a brand's visibility across ChatGPT, Gemini, AI Overviews and other AI models.

### Which of these tools publishes its prices?

Frase, Surfer and Clearscope publish plan prices on their pricing pages. MarketMuse lists plans but points each one to a demo instead of showing a price. Prices vary by billing period for Frase and Surfer, so check whether a figure is yearly or month to month.

### Do any of these tools offer a free trial?

Frase lists a free 7-day trial with no credit card. Clearscope lists a 14 day free trial. Surfer gives 7 days of its Pro plan but takes billing details and does not cancel automatically. MarketMuse has a Free plan with 1 user and 10 queries per month.

### Do I need special optimization to appear in Google's AI Overviews?

Google's documentation says there are no additional requirements to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode, and no other special optimizations are necessary. A page must be indexed and eligible to be shown in Google Search with a snippet.
